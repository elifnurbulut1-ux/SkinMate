♡ Skin Guide ♡

♡ Oily Skin

Produces excess sebum, causing the skin to look shiny. Pores may appear larger, especially around the nose, forehead and chin.

Common Concerns

Blackheads, clogged pores, acne and excess shine.

♡ Dry Skin

Produces less oil than normal and may feel tight, rough or flaky. It can sometimes appear dull and feel uncomfortable after washing.

Common Concerns

Flaking, tightness, rough texture and a weakened skin barrier.

♡ Combination Skin

Has more than one skin type at the same time. The T-zone is usually oily, while the cheeks may be normal or dry.

Common Concerns

Oiliness in the T-zone, dry cheeks, enlarged pores and occasional breakouts.

♡ Normal Skin

Generally well-balanced, with neither too much oil nor excessive dryness. The skin usually feels comfortable and has a relatively even texture.

Common Concerns

Occasional breakouts, mild sensitivity or changes caused by weather and skincare products.

♡ Sensitive Skin

Reacts more easily to certain skincare products, environmental factors or weather changes.

Common Concerns

Redness, itching,irritation and discomfort.
